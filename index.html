UNOFFICIAL COMMUNITY MIRROR

An easier way to try FilmCraft

This site simply makes FilmCraft easier to try without installing it. FilmCraft is developed by the ArtCraft Team and contributors. This mirror is not operated or endorsed by them.

You are loading the official v0.2.1 WebAssembly module with a community bootstrap. The web and desktop apps share an editing engine, but their features are not identical.

  • Media stay local. Pick or drop files; saves and exports download new files. Rust codecs are built in, with no separate FFmpeg download. WebCodecs can accelerate decoding where supported.
  • The official app uses OPFS to recover projects and some media, subject to quota, private browsing and data clearing. Save projects and retain original media; recovery is not a backup.
  • WebGPU or WebGL2 is required. This build is single-threaded and exports use memory. Long videos, 4K and effects can be slow. Proxies, rendered previews, Project Manager and mask tracking are unavailable.
  • Mobile scaling adjusts display size, not touch support. Browser limits do not imply identical desktop limits. FilmCraft remains alpha; the desktop app is not guaranteed to have every feature complete.

Based on the v0.2.1 source, not an exhaustive test of every editing feature. Keep backups and evaluate the official desktop app before production work.
